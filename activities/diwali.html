Step 1 · Watch

Diwali: the Festival of Lights

3½ minutes, with captions on screen. Download the film to show it offline.

Talk about it (before or after the game)
  1. Why do you think so many different faiths use light as a symbol?
  2. Rama, Guru Hargobind and Mahavira have very different stories. What do the stories have in common?
  3. Fireworks are fun but bad for the air. What would you suggest to a city council at Diwali?
  4. Thailand celebrates Loy Krathong with lights on water. How is it similar to Diwali, and how is it different?
