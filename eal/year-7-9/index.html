Units use the topics of Mindset for IELTS Foundation (Cambridge) as everyday English, not as exam preparation. Levels are guides for teaching, not test results.

Course goals

Every unit practises the same four Can Do goals at three levels: Levels 1–2, Levels 3–4 and Levels 5–6.

ListenUnderstand key information, main ideas and details in what people say.
ReadFind answers, main ideas and supporting details in short texts.
WriteComplete sentences, then write paragraphs in order, with time words.
SpeakAnswer questions, tell events in order and build on a partner’s ideas.
