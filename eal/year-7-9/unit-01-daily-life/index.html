Year 7–9 EAL · Unit 1

My Day

Talk, read, listen and write about what you do every day.

Take the Unit 1 Challenge View the Unit 1 slides (PDF) Print the worksheets (PDF)

Book: Mindset for IELTS Foundation, Unit 1 Daily Life, pages 10–19.

In this unit we learn

  • Words for my day
  • make, do, have, go
  • Telling the time
  • I play / She plays · How often?
  • Reading about students’ days
  • Listening: what people do
  • Writing about my day
  • Talking about my day

Our Can Do goals

Levels 1–2Levels 3–4Levels 5–6
ListenI can point to the activity I hear.I can find the main idea and details.I can put events in order.
ReadI can find answers to What? When? Where?I can find the main idea of a paragraph.I can find the central idea and details.
WriteI can complete sentences with a word bank.I can write my day in order with time words.I can show changes in time.
SpeakI can answer Wh- questions about my day.I can tell my day in order.I can ask questions and add to ideas.

Tips in this unit

  • Reading. Check all the texts before you answer. (Book p. 13)
  • Listening. People may use different words with the same meaning. Listen for the final answer. (Book p. 16)
  • Writing. Use always, usually, sometimes or never to say how often. (Book p. 14)
  • Speaking. Plan with short words, not full sentences. (Book p. 16)

WIDA focus: Recount (listening, reading, writing, speaking) and Discuss (pair talk), Levels 1–6 (WIDA Can Do Descriptors, Key Uses Edition, Grades 6–8)
