IELTS strategies in this unit

  • Listening. Before you listen, predict what kind of information is missing (a date, time, age, name or noun). Write numbers as figures (4, not four) to avoid spelling mistakes. (Tip 04, p. 81)
  • Reading. Skim the text first to understand the main ideas. (Tip 14, p. 84)
  • Reading. For True / False / Not Given: read the statement carefully, highlight the key words, find the right paragraph, then scan for the key words and their synonyms (young people = teenagers). If the information is not in the text, the answer is Not Given. (Exam strategy and Tip 15, p. 86)
  • Speaking. Do not give short answers. Give reasons with because and examples with for example or like. (box, p. 83)
